# Code of conduct

Be respectful, precise, and constructive. Discuss the work, not the worth of
the person doing it. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, deliberate
credential exposure, and publication of another party's private code or
security report are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and restrict participation when
needed to protect people or the project. Report conduct concerns through a
private maintainer channel rather than escalating them in public. When the
repository is published, its owners should document a monitored private contact
in the hosting platform.

Good-faith disagreement about threat models, evidence, and tradeoffs is welcome.
Assume curiosity, show reproducible evidence, and give people room to correct
mistakes.
